Frequently Asked Questions

Kya payment safe hai?

Haan, hum Razorpay use karte hain — India ka most trusted payment gateway. Sab cards/UPI/wallets supported hain. ShubhCard tera card data store nahi karta.

Subscription kaise cancel karu?

Kabhi bhi cancel kar sakte ho — Account settings mein "Manage Subscription" pe ja kar. Cancel karne ke baad bhi running month ke end tak Premium features active rahenge.

Refund milta hai?

Haan, 7 din ke andar full refund milega agar tu satisfied nahi hai. Details Refund Policy mein hain.

Free version mein kya restrictions hain?

Free mein sab core features hain, bas PDFs pe ek small "ShubhCard" watermark dikhta hai aur Premium templates lock hote hain. 15+ standard templates fully usable hain.

Kya ek baar pay karke lifetime use kar sakte hain?

Abhi monthly subscription model hai (₹99/mo). Future mein lifetime/annual plans aa sakte hain.
