Refund Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

TL;DR: Hum 7 din ki "no questions asked" refund policy offer karte hain. Agar aap satisfied nahi hain — full refund milega within 7-10 business days.

1. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund of your ShubhCard Pro subscription if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial purchase
  • This is your first subscription (one-time refund per user account)
  • You have not violated our Terms of Service

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  • Send an email to support.shubhcard@gmail.com from your registered email address
  • Subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Email]"
  • Briefly explain the reason (helps us improve, but not mandatory)
  • Include your Razorpay Payment ID (optional, speeds up processing)

3. Refund Processing Time

  • Approval: Within 24-48 hours of receiving your request
  • Refund Initiation: Immediately after approval
  • Bank Credit: 7-10 business days (depending on your bank/UPI provider)

Refunds are processed via Razorpay to the original payment method (card, UPI, wallet).

4. What Happens After Refund

Once your refund is approved:

  • Your Premium access will be revoked immediately
  • You can continue using the Free version
  • Your account and saved documents will not be deleted (you can re-subscribe anytime)

5. Non-Refundable Cases

We reserve the right to deny refunds in the following situations:

  • Refund requested after 7 days of purchase
  • Account has been suspended for Terms of Service violations
  • Repeated refund requests on different accounts (potential abuse)
  • Subscription was already cancelled and reactivated multiple times

6. Subscription Renewals

The 7-day refund window applies to your initial purchase only, not to monthly renewals. However:

  • You can cancel anytime to prevent future renewals
  • You will continue to have Premium access until the end of the current billing cycle
  • No partial refunds for unused days within an active billing cycle

7. Pro-rated Refunds (Special Cases)

In rare cases involving:

  • Service outages exceeding 48 hours
  • Permanent removal of a feature you specifically paid for
  • Wrongful charges or duplicate payments

We may offer pro-rated refunds beyond the 7-day window. Contact support for evaluation.

8. Free Trial

ShubhCard's Free tier serves as our trial — you can use the app without paying to evaluate features before subscribing. We strongly recommend testing the Free version first.

9. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback through your bank without first contacting our support team, we reserve the right to:

  • Suspend your account permanently
  • Dispute the chargeback with evidence of service usage
  • Recover any associated fees

Please always contact us first — we resolve 99% of issues amicably.

10. Contact for Refunds

Refund Requests:

Email: support.shubhcard@gmail.com

Subject: "Refund Request"

Response Time: Within 24-48 hours

Our goal is to make refunds painless and fast.
