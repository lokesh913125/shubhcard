Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

ShubhCard ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our service at shubhcard.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and password (encrypted). If you sign in with Google, we collect your Google profile name and email.

Document Data: Information you enter while creating documents (resume content, biodata details, invoice data) is stored in your browser locally and optionally synced to our servers if you have a Premium account.

Payment Information: When you subscribe to Premium, payment is processed through Razorpay. We do NOT store your credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs on our servers. Razorpay handles all payment data securely as per PCI-DSS standards.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics — pages visited, features used, browser type, device type — to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the ShubhCard service
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To send important service updates (e.g., subscription renewal reminders)
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To improve features and user experience
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

3. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): For authentication and database storage. Firebase is GDPR-compliant.
  • Razorpay: For processing payments. They are PCI-DSS certified.
  • Vercel: Our hosting provider for the website.
  • Legal Authorities: Only if required by Indian law or court order.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted password storage (Firebase Auth)
  • HTTPS/SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure payment processing via Razorpay
  • Regular security audits

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Use the service at your own risk.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will:

  • Delete your personal information within 30 days
  • Retain anonymous usage data for analytics
  • Retain payment records as required by Indian tax laws (typically 7 years)

6. Your Rights

As a ShubhCard user, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your documents in standard formats
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

To exercise these rights, email us at support.shubhcard@gmail.com.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your work-in-progress documents and preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. Authentication cookies are used to keep you logged in (managed by Firebase).

8. Children's Privacy

ShubhCard is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

9. Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

10. International Users

ShubhCard is operated from India. By using our service, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in India under Indian privacy laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified via email. Continued use of the service indicates acceptance.

12. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data requests:

Email: support.shubhcard@gmail.com

Location: Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, India

We respond to privacy requests within 7 business days.
